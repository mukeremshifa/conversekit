02

How it works

Three steps, and the third one never ends

Onboarding a client is a row in a table and a script tag. There is no per-customer deploy, no rebuild, and nothing to install on their side.

Step 1

Tell it about the business

Name, address, hours, payment and cancellation policy, the links you want offered. All of it filled in once, in a form. Longer material becomes a knowledge source: chunked, embedded and searched at question time.

Open the dashboard
Step 2

Paste one tag

The snippet goes anywhere before the closing body tag. The widget styles itself from the bot's own brand colour and only answers on the origins you allow.

See the snippet
Step 3

It answers, and it listens

Replies stream token by token. When a visitor shows intent the model collects a name, email or phone number mid-conversation, and every transcript stays readable afterwards.

Under the hood

Built for someone running this for other people

One bot or fifty, for your own site or for a roster of clients: each one stays walled off from the others, stays yours to adjust, and starts answering the moment someone asks.

Every client kept apart

Conversations, documents and settings never cross accounts, and a bot answers only on the sites you name.

Yours to tune and to check

Answers that start straight away

Replies arrive as they are written, a few words at a time. Nobody sits watching a spinner.
