# Practice Policies

## Cancellations

We ask for at least 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel or move an
appointment. A missed appointment without notice is charged at 30 pounds,
and at 50 pounds for appointments longer than an hour.

## Payment and insurance

We accept cash, all major debit and credit cards, and bank transfer. We do
not accept cheques. We are not an NHS practice, so all treatment is private.
We work with most major dental insurance providers including Denplan, Bupa
Dental and Simplyhealth, and we can supply an itemised receipt for you to
claim back.

## New patients

New patients are welcome and there is currently no waiting list. Your first
visit is a full assessment including x-rays where clinically needed, and a
written treatment plan with costs before anything is booked.

## Children

We see children from the age of two. Children under 18 registered with a
parent who is a patient here are seen for examinations at no charge.

## Complaints

If something has gone wrong, ask to speak to the practice manager. We
respond to written complaints within five working days.
