# Treatment Prices

All prices are in pounds and include VAT. We do not charge for a first
consultation.

## Routine care

A standard dental examination costs 45 pounds and takes about twenty minutes.
A scale and polish with the hygienist costs 80 pounds. Booking both together
on the same visit costs 110 pounds rather than 125.

## Cosmetic treatment

In-practice teeth whitening costs 199 pounds for a full course. Take-home
whitening trays cost 149 pounds and include two rounds of gel. Composite
bonding is priced per tooth at 180 pounds.

## Restorative treatment

A white filling costs between 95 and 160 pounds depending on the size of the
cavity. A single dental implant starts at 1200 pounds including the crown.
Root canal treatment on a front tooth costs 350 pounds; on a molar it costs
550 pounds because the procedure takes considerably longer.

## Orthodontics

Clear aligner treatment varies by case and is quoted after an assessment.
Most adult cases fall between 2200 and 3400 pounds. We offer interest-free
payment plans over twelve months on any treatment above 500 pounds.
