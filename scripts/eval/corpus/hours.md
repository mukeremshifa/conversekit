# Opening Hours and Finding Us

## When we are open

We are open Monday to Thursday from 8am to 6pm, and on Friday from 8am to
4pm. We are open on the first Saturday of each month from 9am to 1pm for
routine appointments only. We are closed every Sunday and on all bank
holidays.

## Emergencies

If you have a dental emergency outside these hours, call the practice number
and the answerphone will give you the out-of-hours service. Emergency
appointments are usually offered the same day if you call before 10am.

## Where we are

The practice is at 14 Marchmont Street, two minutes' walk from the station.
There is no car park on site, but the multi-storey on Cardigan Street is a
four-minute walk and is free after 6pm.

## Accessibility

The ground floor surgery is fully step-free and there is a wheelchair
accessible toilet. Please tell us when booking if you need the ground floor
room, as it is the only one without stairs.
